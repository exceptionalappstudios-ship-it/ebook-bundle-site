Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data SeekhoShop (seekhoshop.com) collects, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. We follow India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

What we collect

  • When you buy: our checkout partner SuperProfile (by Cosmofeed) collects your name, email, phone number and order details, and shares them and your payment status with us. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by SuperProfile and its payment partners. We never see or store them.
  • When you browse: we use the Meta Pixel, which uses cookies and similar technology to record things like pages you view, buttons you tap, your browser and device type, and your IP address.
  • When you contact us: your email address and whatever you write to us.

Why we use it

  • To deliver your purchase and send your download links.
  • To answer your questions and fix download problems.
  • To measure and improve our Facebook and Instagram ads, and show ads to people likely to be interested.
  • To prevent fraud and meet tax, accounting and legal duties.

We do not sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

  • SuperProfile (Cosmofeed): checkout, payments and file delivery.
  • Meta Platforms: through the Meta Pixel on this site and the Meta Conversions API on the checkout page. For ad measurement, checkout details such as your email or phone number may be shared in a hashed (scrambled) form.
  • Hostinger: hosts this website. Google: serves our fonts and stores the download files on Google Drive.
  • Government authorities, only when the law requires it.

Cookies and ad choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. To control how Meta uses activity from websites like ours, open Facebook or Instagram and go to Settings → Accounts Centre → Your information and permissions → Your activity off Meta technologies, or Ad preferences.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires. Support emails are kept for up to 2 years. Pixel data is kept by Meta under its own policies.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a summary of your data, correct or complete it, erase it (unless the law requires us to keep it), withdraw your consent, or nominate someone to act for you. Email help@seekhoshop.com. We will acknowledge within 48 hours and respond within 30 days.

Children

Our products are for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

This site uses HTTPS. We don’t store payment details, and only the people who run SeekhoShop can access order information.

Grievance Officer

For any privacy concern or complaint, contact our Grievance Officer at help@seekhoshop.com. See the Contact page for full details.
